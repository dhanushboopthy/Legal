How it works

  1. 1

    Step 1: Send your case

    Fill in the case details and upload the papers: PDF, Word or photos. Pay the review fee online. The amount is shown before you pay.

  2. 2

    Step 2: The advocate reviews it

    The advocate reads the case and tells you whether they can take it on.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Discuss the case

    Write to the advocate and share more documents in the case’s messages, all in one place.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Get the draft

    The advocate sends the draft with its drafting charges. Pay them and download the draft. If something needs changing, say what, and a new draft follows.

Common questions

Who can use this?
Lawyers enrolled with a State Bar Council. You’ll be asked for your enrolment number when you create an account, and the office approves each new account.
How do I pay?
Online through Razorpay, by UPI, card or net banking. Every amount is shown before you pay.
Who can see my case?
Only you and the office. A case you haven’t sent yet is visible to you alone.
